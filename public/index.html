There's a place in New York City where landlords get cited for rats and mice at more than five times the rate of the least-affected borough.

This comes from NYC Open Data's HPD Housing Maintenance Code Violations dataset, covering every violation issued citywide from January 2020 through today, across all five boroughs.

I counted every violation citing rodents, rats, or mice for each borough, then divided by that borough's 2020 Census population to get a fair rate per 100,000 residents.
